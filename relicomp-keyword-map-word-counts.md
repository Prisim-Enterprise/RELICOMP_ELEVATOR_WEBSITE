## Word count logic
- Word counts scale with the difficulty and breadth of the primary keyword's SERP: high-volume/high-SD pages (home elevators, capsule) need comprehensive coverage; low-SD service/industry pages rank with focused 700–1,200-word content.
- Never pad. A 900-word page that fully answers intent beats a 1,800-word page of filler.
- Every product page: specs table + price-range indication + 3–5 FAQs (FAQ schema).
